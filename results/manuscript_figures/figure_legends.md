## Fig. 1 Tumour-conditioned enteric glia define the perturbation-derived programme scaffold

**a** GSE231802 design. Mouse enteric glia received tumour-conditioned medium, healthy-tissue-conditioned medium or control medium for 6, 12 and 24 hours, with four repository-designated replicate libraries per treatment-time group. **b** Time-specific limma log2 fold changes for genes that increased in tumour-conditioned cells at two or more time points. Mouse gene symbols label rows. **c** Restriction of 33 time-consistent genes to 13 Ensembl-mapped human genes measured by both GSE303070 CosMx panel versions. The derivation criterion required a log2 fold change above 0.5 and a Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery rate below 0.05 at two or more time points

## Fig. 2 Nine CRC Atlas studies validate the convergent human module

**a** Depth-adjusted TIMP1-IFITM1-CHI3L1 scores in matched primary-tumour and adjacent-normal Schwann-cell compartments from 44 patients across nine CRC Atlas studies. Lines connect tissues from the same patient. The hierarchical-bootstrap interval sampled studies and then patients within studies. The *P* value comes from a two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test. **b** Median paired tumour-normal differences for each study. Symbol area scales with the number of paired patients. **c** Median paired differences across minimum requirements of 3, 5, 10 and 20 Schwann cells per tissue sample. Error bars show hierarchical-bootstrap 95% intervals; labels give the number of positive patients over the number tested. The note reports leave-one-study-out results at the primary five-cell threshold

## Fig. 3 Paired CosMx pseudobulk supports the cross-platform scaffold

**a** Thirteen-gene pseudobulk scores in matched tumour and normal Schwann-cell compartments from eight GSE303070 donors. Lines connect tissues from the same donor. The interval shows a percentile interval from 200,000 paired bootstrap samples; the *P* value comes from an exact two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test. **b** Donor-level tumour-normal log1p counts-per-million differences for each scaffold gene. **c** Median paired differences and Benjamini-Hochberg *q* values for all 13 genes. *TIMP1* and *IFITM1* increase in all eight pairs, while *CHI3L1* increases in seven; each reaches *q* = 0.0508. *MT2A* decreases in seven pairs

## Fig. 4 Supportive cohorts converge while spatial adjustment defines the boundary

**a, b** Patient-level 13-gene scaffold scores in published enteric-glial cells from GSE132465 and GSE144735. Each point represents one patient-tissue summary; point area scales with the number of enteric-glial cells. Horizontal bars show group medians, and grey lines connect tissues from the same patient. **c** Descriptive Hedges *g* values across minimum inclusion thresholds of 1, 3, 5 and 10 enteric-glial cells per summary. Mixed paired and unpaired contributions preclude inferential intervals. The single GSE132465 tumour summary at the ten-cell threshold precludes a standardized difference. **d** Specimen-level standardized associations between the 13-gene score and epithelial fraction among 20 nearest neighbours in 15 tumour specimens. Diamonds show medians. The adjusted model includes library size, S100B, myeloid, fibroblast and epithelial transcriptional burdens and PanCK. The adjusted median has a specimen-bootstrap 95% interval, and the field-of-view permutation *P* value uses 2,000 permutations
